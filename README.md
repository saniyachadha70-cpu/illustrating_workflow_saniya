## **Comparative Analysis of Transposable Elements in Trichinella spiralis and T8**

**Overview**: This project investigates patterns of retrotransposon insertions and deletions in Trichinella spiralis and the African Trichinella T8 genotype. By comparing transposable element (TE) composition and activity between these closely related parasitic nematodes, the study explores whether TE dynamics may contribute to differences in host adaptation

**Research Question:** Do differences in retrotransposon insertion and deletion patterns between T. spiralis and T8 reveal patterns in how each organism adapts to its host?

**Workflow:**

1.  Import TE annotation datasets.

2.  Filter annotations to retain retrotransposon classes (LTR, LINE, SINE, PLE).

3.  Generate boxplots comparing: Insertion percentages, Deletion percentages

4.  Combine datasets to compare species-level patterns.

5.  Calculate relative retrotransposon composition.

6.  Perform a chi-squared test to evaluate compositional differences between genomes.

**Key Findings:**

- T. spiralis and T8 exhibit highly similar retrotransposon insertion and deletion profiles.

- Overall TE composition appears conserved between the two genomes.

- TE composition alone does not explain differences in host adaptation.

- Future studies should investigate the genomic locations of TEs rather than only their abundance
